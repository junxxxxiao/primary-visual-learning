When the same string keeps the same length, thickness, and tension, plucking it harder mainly increases vibration amplitude. A larger amplitude makes the sound louder. It does not by itself make the vibration frequency higher, so pitch does not directly become higher.

[page 13]
Pitch describes how high or low a sound seems and is related to vibration frequency. Loudness describes how strong or weak a sound seems and is related here to vibration amplitude. A transfer check may compare the same tuning fork struck with different force while holding the object fixed.
